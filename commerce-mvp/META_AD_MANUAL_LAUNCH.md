# SANTRAM INDUSTRIES — Meta B2B test campaign (MANUAL PLAN, NOT LIVE)

**Budget:** ₹300/day × 7 days = **₹2,100 before tax/fees**.
**Objective (where available):** Engagement → Messaging Apps → WhatsApp.
**Locations:** Tiruppur and Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.
**Buyers:** garment manufacturers, apparel wholesalers, boutique owners and apparel sourcing teams. Check actual targeting options in the advertiser's Ads Manager; do not assume a specific job/interest targeting option is available.
**Creative:** 3 customer-approved, watermark-safe promotional images. Do not use technical production masters or original customer-owned logos.
**CTA:** Send WhatsApp Message.
**Conversion event:** Qualified enquiry that provides product type, garment quantity, city and request. NOT simply an impression or accidental WhatsApp click.

## Three original text concepts for testing

A. **Premium decorative finishes:** "आपके गारमेंट के लिए अलग पहचान। Rhinestone, velvet और premium hotfix concepts — bulk manufacturing enquiries के लिए संपर्क करें।" 
B. **Manufacturer speed:** "Shirt & T-shirt decoration buyers — custom rhinestone / DTF / patch concepts. Quantity और requirement भेजें; हमारी टीम quotation पर चर्चा करेगी।"
C. **Design approvals:** "Exclusive garment embellishment previews for brands and manufacturers. Request a private catalogue and a manufacturing quotation on WhatsApp."

## Manual 7-day review protocol

Day 1: verify connected Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, WhatsApp Business and billing; check status in Meta Ads Manager; record start date/time and spending limit.
Days 2–3: record spend, reach, conversations started, *qualified* enquiries, and quote requests separately. Do not optimize based on clicks alone.
Days 4–5: compare 3 creatives and geographic response, reject irrelevant leads; avoid drastic daily changes that interfere with learning.
Day 7: compute cost per qualified lead and number of quotations/confirmed orders; present a human-approved budget and creative change proposal.

**Approval gate:** Never auto-increase budget, launch campaigns, publish product prices or confirm orders. Pause decisions can be suggested, but require authorized Meta integration and operator confirmation.
