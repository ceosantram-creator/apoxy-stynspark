Privacy Notice / गोपनीयता सूचना

PRE-LAUNCH DRAFT: business owner must approve this notice and add verified legal contact details before any public launch.

What we collect / कौन-सा डेटा

Buyer name, phone, city, quantity, brand, requirement/message, consent, enquiry date and manually updated lead/order status. Public page views are counted without storing IP addresses in the application database. The infrastructure provider may maintain its own access logs.

Why / उद्देश्य

To review and respond to B2B product enquiries, prepare draft quotations, and track manually authorized business orders. Public enquiry submission is not an accepted order and is not consent to unsolicited advertising.

Sharing / साझा करना

No automatic transfer to Meta Ads, WhatsApp Cloud API, payment providers or an external CRM is implemented in this MVP. Hosting providers may process data necessary to run the service. No Meta Pixel or CAPI tracking is active.

Access, deletion and retention / अधिकार

Only a logged-in administrator can inspect buyer details. Request access, correction or deletion through the business contact to be added before launch. Data retention rules, applicable exceptions for accounting/orders, and the grievance/contact mechanism must be approved before publishing.

Security / सुरक्षा

Session cookies, app approval gates and protected routes are included, but security depends on HTTPS hosting, restricted access, backup encryption, monitoring and administrator policy.

Owner approval missing / लॉन्च रोकने के कारण

Registered business contact, controller information, privacy policy approval, retention schedule, hosting processor terms, breach handling and applicable Indian law compliance review are still pending.
